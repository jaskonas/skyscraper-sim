# Plan 11 — Crowns Rebuilt Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Every one of the 28 crowns is rebuilt as real architecture, modelled on the building named in its `seenOn`. Seen up close, each crown has the features a person would name when describing that building: tiers, ribs, arches, windows, pinnacles, finials, lattices and lights.

**Architecture:**
- **Today's stage-built crowns stay** as the *simple* version. They are used on Detail: Simple, in the sidebar icons, and for the roof deck.
- **A new *rich* builder for every crown** lives in `src/scene/crowns/rich/`. It is built from a small kit of architectural helpers and a few new part shapes. It is used on Detail: High.
- **Small pieces are marked `fine`** (crockets, rivets, mullions, small ribs, lights). They are drawn only while the camera is within 300 m of the crown.
- **Nothing in the analysis changes:** crown heights, official height and the landmark flag all come from the catalog as before.

**Tech Stack:** React 19, three 0.186, @react-three/fiber 9, Vitest 5.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-20-surface-detail-design.md` §6 (and §2, §8). The user's request: "add a lot more architectural detail to things like the spires".

## Global Constraints

- No new dependencies.
- **Never push or merge to `master` without the user's explicit word.**
- **Part budget per rich crown:** at most **2,500** parts, of which at most **700** are not `fine`.
- **Bounds:** every rich crown, on every plan shape and at both extreme designs, must:
  - be finite;
  - stand on the roof (no part below `body − 0.5 m`);
  - stay within `0.6 × hypot(W, D) + 1` of the axis;
  - reach the catalog tip height within `max(5% H, 0.5 m)`.

  (Same rules as today's `crownParts` test.)
- **Open-roof crowns** (flat, cornice, concrete-cap, twin-antennas, lattice-mast, wind-turbines) keep their deck where it is. `roofDeck(design)` reads the *simple* crown, and the rich crown's top-of-slab must match it within 0.05 m.
- **Ornament amount None** drops decorative `fine` parts but keeps structural ones. Crown builders receive `ornament` in the context and decide.
- Glowing parts glow at night, as today. Blinking aircraft lights stay on masts and spires.

## Review Focus

1. **Twisted, tapered or triangle-topped towers:** rich crowns sit on the turned top floor and fit its plan. Test in Task 2 (the bounds test runs on all shapes and on the twist-120 extreme).
2. **The sidebar's crown gallery stays fast:** icons use the simple crowns. Test in Task 2 (`CrownIcon` parts equal the simple crown).
3. **Zooming out from a detailed crown:** the fine parts disappear without leaving holes, because the coarse parts carry the silhouette. Test in Task 7 (with fine parts hidden, the coarse parts still reach the tip).
4. **Construction animation:** the crown appears from the bottom up as today, using the rich crown on High. Test in Task 7 (`upTo` still reveals all rich parts at share 1).
5. **Wind and quake sway:** rich crowns bend with the tower. This is covered by the existing `PartsMesh` sway; Task 7 renders a swaying rich crown.

### Task 2: The kit, the rich/simple switch, and the bounds harness

**Files:**
- Create: `src/scene/crowns/kit.ts`, `src/scene/crowns/rich/index.ts`
- Modify: `src/scene/crowns/types.ts` (`CrownContext` adds `ornament: Ornament`), `src/scene/crownParts.ts`, `src/scene/Building.tsx`, `src/scene/detail/detailModel.ts`, `src/scene/dressing/dressing.ts`
- Test: `src/scene/crowns/kit.test.ts`, `src/scene/crownParts.test.ts`

**Interfaces:**
- Produces:
  - `crownParts(design: Design, level: 'simple' | 'rich' = 'simple'): Part[]`. `'rich'` uses `RICH_CROWNS[id]` when present; otherwise it falls back to simple.
  - `RICH_CROWNS: Partial<Record<CrownId, CrownBuilder>>` in `rich/index.ts`, filled in by later tasks.
  - **Kit** (all in the crown's own frame, with y = 0 at the roof):
    - `ring(n: number, radius: number, y: number, make: (angle: number, i: number) => Part | Part[]): Part[]`: copies placed round a circle, each facing out (yaw = angle).
    - `aroundPlan(ctx, inset: number, spacing: number, y: number, make: (a: Anchor) => Part | Part[]): Part[]`: along the plan's walls, via `perimeterPoints`, shrunk by `inset`.
    - `atCorners(ctx, spread: number, make: (c: Anchor) => Part | Part[]): Part[]`
    - `finial(x, y, z, h, material): Part[]`: a ball, a neck, a point; 3 parts, with the point `fine`.
    - `pinnacle(x, y, z, h, w, material, crockets: number): Part[]`: an octagon shaft, a gable band, a spire cone and a finial. Crockets are small cones up the spire's edges, all `fine`.
    - `lattice(y0, y1, r0, r1, sides, bays, thickness, material): Part[]`: vertical chords, a ring per bay and X-diagonals per face and bay (struts via `strutPart`). Diagonals are `fine`.
    - `tier(ctx, y0, y1, w, material, opts?: { windows?: number; band?: boolean }): Part[]`: a plan slab, with an optional moulding band on top and optional lit glass windows round it (`fine`, glow).
    - `ribs(n, radius0, radius1, y0, y1, thickness, material): Part[]`: vertical ribs from y0 to y1, tapering from r0 to r1.
  - **`CrownIcon`** keeps calling `crownParts(d)` (simple).
  - **`roofDeck`** keeps reading `crownParts(design)` (simple).
  - **`Building`** uses `crownParts(design, facadeDetail ? 'rich' : 'simple')`.
  - **The detail roof view** uses `'rich'`.

    - `lattice(0, 20, 3, 1, 4, 5, 0.2, 'steel')` gives 4 chords and 5 rings, with diagonals all `fine`, and every part within radius 3.2.
